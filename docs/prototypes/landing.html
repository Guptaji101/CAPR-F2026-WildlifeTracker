How it works

1

Pick a place

Type a city or park, tap a preset like Busan or Jeju, or press “Locate Me”.

2

See what lives there

Real sightings appear as colored pins within your chosen radius — 5 to 100 km.

3

Tap to learn more

Open any pin for the species photo, taxonomy, date and a link to the GBIF record.

A map anyone can read

Built for hikers, students and eco-clubs — not just researchers. No sign-up, no jargon: just the animals recorded near the places you care about.
